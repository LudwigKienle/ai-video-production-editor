# Growth Ideas

Ideas for growing AI Video Production Editor (AVPE), derived from how
Higgsfield AI markets itself. They are adapted to an open-source project: no
bought hype, no money promises. This page complements
`tutorial-video-playbook.md` (video format) and `ludwig-intro-reel.md`
(founder intro).

Research date: 24 Oct 2026. Sources: higgsfield.ai (home, MCP, Claude video
generator, creator partnership program, contests, academy), Higgsfield's
YouTube channel, Higgsfield's own revenue post, a third-party growth analysis
(okara.ai), and Forbes reporting (11 Feb 2026). Company figures are
self-reported run rates, not audited revenue.

## What actually drove Higgsfield

| Lever | Evidence | What it means for us |
| --- | --- | --- |

**Where they got burned (don't copy).** Forbes reported a creator media kit
that contained stock templates presented as AI output, paid share offers
($200 per post), offensive and deepfake promo clips sent to creators, and an
X account suspension for "inauthentic behaviour". Their co-founder said
"controversial content gets attention". This is our contrast: honest, open
and disclosed.

## Ideas, ranked

### Quick wins (this month)

1. **An MCP recipe gallery.** A docs page and an in-app list of copyable Claude
   prompts, each one a named recipe: `/script-to-storyboard`, `/microdrama`,
   `/intro-reel` (Kumar style), `/product-ad`, `/trailer-cut`,
   `/same-shot-every-model`. Each recipe shows the result, the prompt and the
   project file. This is the most direct copy of their MCP page, and it suits
   us better because Claude is our native driver.
2. **List the MCP server everywhere.** Submit it to the Claude connectors
   directory, the official MCP registry and other MCP catalogues, and to
   "awesome MCP" lists. Every listing is a permanent acquisition channel.
3. **Day-one model posts.** Every model added (FLUX.3, Krea 3, GPT Image 2
   this month) gets a 20-second clip: "now in the open-source editor, bring
   your own key". Generate it from the release notes.
4. **German-language content.** Germany is Higgsfield's third-largest market
   and there is little German AI-filmmaking tutorial content. Post every
   video in German and English; YouTube multi-language audio is enough.
5. **A transparency pledge in the README.** "No paid posts without
   disclosure. Every demo states its models and costs, including failed
   takes." It costs nothing and contrasts with the Forbes story.

### Community engine (next 1–2 months)

6. **AVPE Creators**, the open-source version of their program. It offers
   early builds, a direct Discord line, a feature on our channels and README,
   co-authored recipes and presets, and provider-credit grants when a
   sponsor provides them. It asks for one public project per month, with the
   project file.
7. **Weekly challenge plus a quarterly Open Film Festival.** Use one theme per
   week ("impossible camera", "one-location thriller", "€0 local-models
   only"). Prizes: a feature in the next video and the README, a contributor
   badge, and sponsored provider credits if a provider (fal, Replicate, BFL,
   Krea) supplies them. Every entry ships its project file.
8. **Open project gallery.** A "Share project" export, a GitHub Discussions
   category "Show your build", and a gallery page that renders entries with
   prompts and references. This is their "see every prompt" idea, but open.
9. **Preset packs as content.** Camera moves, looks and shot templates as
   JSON packs, contributed by pull request. Each pack is a post, and each
   contributor brings their audience.
10. **"Hire an AVPE creator" board.** Their "jobs coming soon" points to
    demand. A simple free directory of community filmmakers who work with
    AVPE helps both sides and keeps creators around.

### Education funnel

11. **AVPE Academy:** "Your first AI film in 90 minutes" in 7 short lessons,
    built on the demo project. The homework runs entirely on local models, so
    it is free, and a shareable certificate (LinkedIn) creates the loop. Put
    it under GitHub Pages and YouTube.

### Reach and authority

12. **SEO pages on GitHub Pages:** "open source AI video editor", "Claude AI
    video editor (MCP)", "local AI storyboard generator", "free AI filmmaking
    software", "Seedance 2.5 storyboard workflow", "Midjourney to video
    storyboard", "Blender for AI filmmaking", and an honest comparison page
    ("hosted AI suites vs. open source"). Each page needs an FAQ and real
    screenshots.
13. **AVPE Originals:** OWNED as a series, with a behind-the-scenes episode
    for each and the full project open. Their originals are their biggest
    reach driver.
14. **Case studies with productions.** Real hybrid workflows (for example an
    environment swap for TV, if the production agrees) presented as "how a
    production used open-source AI tooling". This is the agency angle that
    made Higgsfield its revenue, here used for credibility and contributors.
15. **Guest challenges:** an AI-assisted workflow against a traditional VFX
    or edit workflow, with a guest artist, so both audiences see it.

## Measure

For each idea, track stars and clones within 48 hours of the post, MCP
directory referrals, recipe-page visits, challenge entries, and German
versus English watch time. Repeat what moves stars and active projects, not
views alone.
